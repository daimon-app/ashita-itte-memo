# M03 Market / Competition / Pricing

## Product position
Not a general to-do list. The product ritual is: decide tomorrow's single concrete first move at the end of work, then see it immediately the next morning.

## Competitive frame
- General task managers (Todoist etc.) compete on projects, filters, reminders and collaboration. Their strength is breadth; that breadth is also the opposite of M03's deliberate simplicity.
- Minimal single-focus apps exist. OneThink markets one daily focus with a free tier and paid Pro/lifetime options. Apple's Japan App Store also lists a minimalist 'One - Do One Thing Today' built around choosing tomorrow's most important task at night; it is priced at ¥1,500 at the time of audit.

## Differentiation that is supportable by current implementation
- end-of-work -> next-morning ritual
- one concrete first move rather than an expandable task list
- local-first data
- history/calendar review
- backup export/import
- field/work mode wording

Do not claim uniqueness, scientific productivity improvement, guaranteed results, or features not verified in the shipping build.

## Price decision
Launch recommendation: **¥500 one-time purchase** for v1.

Reasoning:
- low enough for a narrow utility with no cloud account or subscription service
- materially below the ¥1,500 minimalist competitor observed in Japan
- avoids recurring-fee resistance for a simple local-first tool
- preserves room for later bundle pricing with other DAIMON utilities without confusing M03 with P02

## Pricing gate
PASS for sales-foundation purposes at ¥500 one-time, subject to actual store/payment fees, tax handling, and owner approval at publication.

## Launch KPI
Do not optimize first for ARPU. Track whether buyers actually create tomorrow's action, return next morning, and continue using the ritual over 7 days.
